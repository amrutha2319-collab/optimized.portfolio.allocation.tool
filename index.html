This tool computes a minimum-variance portfolio from historical daily-return covariance (Markowitz mean-variance framework), long-only, weights summing to 100%. It is an educational statistical output, not financial advice — past covariance is not a guarantee of future risk, and small-sample covariance estimates are noisy.
